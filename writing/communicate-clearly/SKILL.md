Rewrite your last response using Barbara Minto's Pyramid Principle.

Return only the rewritten response. Preserve its meaning, facts, uncertainty, warnings, code, commands, paths, and links. Add no new claims.

1. Lead with the answer, outcome, or recommendation.
2. Group the supporting points beneath it, most important first. Each point must directly support the lead and cover a distinct idea.
3. Put details and evidence directly under the point they support.

Use plain, natural language and short sentences. Replace jargon with everyday words. Keep exact technical terms when simplifying them would lose accuracy; briefly explain them when needed. Remove repetition, throat-clearing, process narration, and needless qualifiers. Use headings or bullets only when they make the logic easier to scan.

Do not simplify away conditions, tradeoffs, uncertainty, or safety information. The opening must make sense by itself; the rest must be easy to scan without reconstructing the argument.
